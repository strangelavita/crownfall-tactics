

========================================

TERRITORY SCORING:
At end of each round:

- Neutral Territory = 10 points
- Enemy Territory = 20 points

Rounds 8, 9, and 10:
All scoring is doubled.

SCORING DISPLAY:
- Floating score indicators
- End-round score breakdown animation
- Territory highlighting based on ownership value

========================================
MATCH STRUCTURE
========================================

TOTAL ROUNDS:
10


ROUND FLOW:

1. Draw Phase
- Both players draw 1 card

2. Player Turn Phase
- Active player gains 3 AP
- Spend AP on actions
- End turn manually

3. Opponent Turn Phase
- Opponent gains 3 AP
- Opponent acts
- End turn

4. Resolution Phase
- Tower damage triggers
- End-of-round effects
- Territory scoring
- Round transition animation

5. Next Round

WIN CONDITION:
Highest score after Round 10 wins.

TIEBREAKER:
1. Most surviving troops
2. Highest total troop health
3. Sudden death extra round

========================================
ACTION POINT SYSTEM
========================================

Each player gains:
3 AP every turn

AP COSTS:
- Deploy troop = 1 AP
- Move = 1 AP
- Attack = 1 AP
- Ability use = 1 AP
- Space card activation = 1 AP

RULES:
- AP resets every turn
- Unused AP does not carry over
- Units normally act once per turn
- Knights are exceptions due to mobility ability

========================================
MOVEMENT RULES
========================================

MOVEMENT:
- Orthogonal only
- No diagonal movement

MOVE RESTRICTIONS:
- One troop per tile
- Cannot move through units
- Cannot move after attacking unless ability allows
- Newly summoned units cannot attack on deployment turn

MOVEMENT VISUALS:
- Highlight legal tiles
- Animated movement path
- Hover previews
