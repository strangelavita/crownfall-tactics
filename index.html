How to Play

🎯 Objective

Play 10 rounds on a 5x4 board. Control territory with surviving troops to score points at the end of each round. Highest score wins, unless a King is slain first.

⚔️ Combat

Deploy troops, move them, and attack enemies. Each action costs AP (Action Points). Player 1 takes a turn with 3 AP, then Player 2 takes a turn with 3 AP.

🏰 Territories

Rows A-B are Player 2's territory, row C is neutral, and rows D-E are Player 1's territory. Own territory scores 5, neutral scores 10, enemy territory scores 20. Rounds 8-10 score double.

👑 The King

Protect your King! If he dies, you lose immediately. A King in own territory gives allied troops in own territory +15 max health. A King pushed into enemy territory empowers allied troops there with +20 attack and +15 max health.

🃏 Space Cards

Before the match, each player places 6 space cards in their own territory. Opponent space cards stay hidden until activated or used. Click one of your space-card tiles during your turn to reveal the activation option.
