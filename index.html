How to Play

🎯 Objective

Control territories to score points. Highest score after 10 rounds wins!

⚔️ Combat

Deploy troops, move them, and attack enemies. Each action costs AP (Action Points).

🏰 Territories

Enemy territory = 20 pts | Neutral = 10 pts | Your territory = 5 pts. Control more = win more!

👑 The King

Protect your King! If he dies, you lose 100 points instantly. He buffs nearby allies.
