Settings

Game Rules

Objective

Play 10 rounds on a 5x4 board. Control territory with surviving troops to score points at the end of each round. Highest score wins, unless a King is slain first.

Round Flow

Each round starts with both players drawing 1 troop card. Player 1 takes a turn with 3 AP, then Player 2 or the AI takes a turn with 3 AP. AP resets every turn and unused AP is lost.

Deck and Hand

Each player has a 15-card troop deck and starts with 5 cards in hand. The first draw phase brings the opening turn hand to 6 cards. Deployed troop cards leave your hand.

AP Costs

Deploying a troop costs that card's AP. Moving costs 1 AP, attacking costs 1 AP, Architect space-card disruption costs 1 AP, and activating any space card costs 1 AP.

Territory Scoring

Rows A-B are the top player's territory, row C is neutral, and rows D-E are the bottom player's territory. Own territory scores 5, neutral scores 10, enemy territory scores 20. Rounds 8-10 score double.

Movement and Attacks

Movement is orthogonal, one troop per tile. Most troops act once per turn. Swordsmen are short-range fighters. Knights can move up to 2 and may move then attack. Archers attack in straight lines through intervening units; Pikemen attack in straight lines only if the path is clear.

Special Troops

The Pope can attempt a 30% conversion on an adjacent non-King, non-Knight troop; converted units are stunned and the Pope has a 2-round cooldown. The Architect can disrupt adjacent visible enemy space cards, disabling permanent cards or destroying unactivated one-time cards.

The King

Protect your King. If your King dies, you lose immediately. A King in own territory gives allied troops in own territory +15 max health. A King pushed into enemy territory empowers allied troops there with +20 attack and +15 max health.

Space Cards

Before the match, each player places 6 space cards in their own territory. Opponent space cards stay hidden until activated or used. Click one of your space-card tiles during your turn to reveal the bottom activation option.

Space Effects

Castle gives a troop on its tile +40 max health. Tower fires 5 damage at adjacent enemies at the start of its owner's turns. Volcano stops a troop from moving this turn and makes it take 50% more damage. Shield blocks the next incoming damage. Rage Potion doubles the next attack. Health Potion restores a troop to full health.

Game Modes

VS AI plays against Easy, Normal, or Hard AI. Quick Match uses the same rules in a 5-round match with round 5 scoring double. Hotseat lets two local players alternate turns on the same device, with each player only seeing their own hidden space cards until cards are activated.
